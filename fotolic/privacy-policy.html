Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Fotolic handles information when you use the app to edit, resize, compress, convert, or export images.

Effective date October 23, 2026Last updated October 31, 2026

Summary

  • Fotolic is designed to process your images on your Apple device.
  • Fotolic does not require an account and does not sell your images or personal information.
  • App Store purchases are processed by Apple through StoreKit.
  • You choose when an image is saved, shared, or exported to another app.
  • Camera, Photos, Notifications, and Tracking permissions are optional. Fotolic asks only when a feature needs them, and you can change your choice at any time in iOS Settings.
01

Scope

This policy applies to the Fotolic iOS application and the Fotolic legal pages linked from the app. “Fotolic,” “we,” “us,” and “our” refer to Sajid A Khokhar, the developer of Fotolic.

02

Information processed

Images and files

When you select a photo, import a file, or take a picture, Fotolic uses that item to perform the operation you request. The current app is designed to perform image processing locally. The image itself is not uploaded to a Fotolic server.

Settings and local app data

Fotolic stores preferences such as output format, quality, metadata options, appearance, haptics, and onboarding state on your device. Temporary working files and exported files are managed by iOS and by the actions you choose.

Support correspondence

If you contact support, we receive the email address and information included in your message. We use that information to respond and maintain a reasonable support record.

03

Use of information

We use information to provide the selected editing or export function, remember your local preferences, process purchases, respond to support requests, maintain the service, and meet legal obligations. We do not use your image content to train models or create advertising profiles.

At the time this policy was published, Fotolic did not require an account and did not use image-content advertising or a remote image-processing service.

04

Permissions

iOS asks for your permission before Fotolic can use certain features of your device. Each permission is optional. Fotolic asks only when a feature needs it, and declining one permission does not stop you from using the rest of the app. You can change any choice later in iOS Settings > Apps > Fotolic.

CameraAsked when you tap Take Photo. Fotolic uses the camera only to take the photo you want to edit. Photos you take stay on your device and are not uploaded. If you decline, you can still choose photos from your library or Files.
Photos (add only)Asked the first time you save an edited image to Photos. Fotolic requests permission to add images only, so it can save your edited copies but cannot see, read, or change the photos already in your library. If you decline, you can still save to Files or share your images.
Photo picker and FilesNo permission is needed. When you choose a photo with the iOS photo picker, or open or save a file with the Files picker, iOS gives Fotolic access only to the items you select.
NotificationsAsked when you first open the app. Fotolic uses notifications only to remind you one day before a Fotolic Pro free trial ends, so you can decide whether to keep the subscription. Reminders are scheduled on your device and are not used for advertising. If you decline, you will not receive the reminder; everything else works the same.
Tracking (App Tracking Transparency)Asked when you first open the app. If you allow tracking, Google Analytics for Firebase may access your device’s advertising identifier (IDFA) to measure which marketing campaigns, such as ads for Fotolic, lead people to install and use the app. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, the advertising identifier is not accessed. Your choice does not affect any feature of the app, and Fotolic shows no ads.
05

Analytics and service providers

Fotolic uses Google Firebase services to understand how the app is used, fix problems, and run the app. These services never receive your photos, files, or the content of your edits.

Firebase AnalyticsRecords how the app is used, such as screens opened, tools used, export counts, and purchase events, together with device model, iOS version, app version, language, and approximate region. The advertising identifier is included only if you allow tracking.
Firebase CrashlyticsSends a crash report if the app stops unexpectedly, including technical details about the device and the app at the time of the crash, so we can fix the problem.
Firebase AuthenticationCreates an anonymous identifier for your installation automatically. It is not linked to your name, email address, or Apple Account, and there is nothing to sign in to.

Google processes this information as our service provider and retains analytics and crash data according to Firebase’s data retention settings. Learn more in Firebase’s privacy information and Google’s Privacy Policy.

06

Purchases and subscriptions

Fotolic Pro purchases are completed through Apple’s App Store and StoreKit. Apple processes payment information and provides the transaction status needed to manage access. Fotolic does not receive your payment card number.

Apple displays the price, billing period, introductory offer, trial eligibility, renewal terms, and cancellation instructions before purchase. Manage or cancel a subscription in your Apple Account settings. Apple’s handling of App Store information is described in Apple’s Privacy Policy.

07

Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or disclose your image content for advertising. When you use Share, Save to Photos, Save to Files, or another export action, iOS transfers the selected output to the destination you choose.

We may disclose support information if required by law, to protect the rights and safety of users, or to address fraud or security issues.

08

Retention and deletion

Your photos, files, exports, and local preferences remain on your device until you delete them, clear the app’s data, or uninstall Fotolic. Support correspondence is retained only as long as reasonably necessary to respond and maintain a support record.

09

Your choices

You can refuse or revoke permissions, delete local files, change settings, and manage subscriptions through iOS. Depending on where you live, you may also have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or information about processing. Contact us to make a request.

10

Children’s privacy

Fotolic is a general-audience photo utility. Its content is suitable for all ages, but the app is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, or under the minimum age set by local law, and we do not use analytics or tracking to target children.

A parent or guardian can turn off tracking for a child’s device in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, for example by contacting support, email us and we will delete it.

11

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when Fotolic’s features or legal requirements change. We will post the revised policy on this page and update the date above. Material changes may also be identified in the app or release notes.
