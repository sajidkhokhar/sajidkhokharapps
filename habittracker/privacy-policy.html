HabitTracker

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 20, 2026

HabitTracker keeps your habits on your device. We don't have accounts, ads or analytics, and we don't collect your data.

Who we are

HabitTracker is an iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch app made by Sajid A Khokhar ("we", "us").

What stays on your device

Everything you enter is stored on your device: habit names, goals, schedules, daily progress, notes, routines, reminders, achievements, your name and profile picture, and your settings. We never receive it, and we can't see it or recover it for you. If you delete the app or erase your data in Settings, it is gone.

Permissions the app can ask for

  • Notifications. Used only for the reminders you set and the optional evening check-in and weekly recap. They are scheduled on your device. You can turn them off in Settings or in iOS.
  • Apple Health (optional). If you connect it, the app reads your steps, exercise minutes, active energy, walking, running, cycling and swimming distance, and recent workouts to show them on the Activity screen. This is read-only: the app never writes to Health. The data is shown on your device and your paired Apple Watch, and is not sent to us or anyone else. You can change access at any time in Settings › Health.
  • Photos (optional). If you choose a profile picture, the app receives only the photo you pick, and saves a copy on your device.

Apple Watch

The Watch app shows today's habits and lets you check them off. Your iPhone and Watch exchange this information directly with each other, using Apple's connection between paired devices. It doesn't pass through our servers.

Purchases

Premium is bought through the App Store. Apple handles your payment and tells the app whether you own Premium. We never see your payment details or Apple Account. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

What we don't do

  • No sign-up or account.
  • No ads and no advertising or tracking SDKs.
  • No analytics or crash reporting that identifies you.
  • We don't sell or share personal information, because we don't have any.

Backups

Your device's own backup (iCloud or a computer) may include the app's data, under Apple's terms. We don't control or access those backups.

Children

The app doesn't collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

Your choices

You can delete everything at any time with Erase All Data in Settings, or by deleting the app. Because we hold no copy, there is nothing for us to export or delete on our side.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will post the new version here with a new date.
