FOTOLIC

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Fotolic handles information when you use the app to edit, resize, compress, convert, or export images.

Effective date October 23, 2026Last updated October 23, 2026

Summary

  • Fotolic is designed to process your images on your Apple device.
  • Fotolic does not require an account and does not sell your images or personal information.
  • App Store purchases are processed by Apple through StoreKit.
  • You choose when an image is saved, shared, or exported to another app.
01

Scope

This policy applies to the Fotolic iOS application and the Fotolic legal pages linked from the app. “Fotolic,” “we,” “us,” and “our” refer to Sajid Khokhar, the developer of Fotolic.

02

Information processed

Images and files

When you select a photo, import a file, or take a picture, Fotolic uses that item to perform the operation you request. The current app is designed to perform image processing locally. The image itself is not uploaded to a Fotolic server.

Settings and local app data

Fotolic stores preferences such as output format, quality, metadata options, appearance, haptics, and onboarding state on your device. Temporary working files and exported files are managed by iOS and by the actions you choose.

Support correspondence

If you contact support, we receive the email address and information included in your message. We use that information to respond and maintain a reasonable support record.

03

Use of information

We use information to provide the selected editing or export function, remember your local preferences, process purchases, respond to support requests, maintain the service, and meet legal obligations. We do not use your image content to train models or create advertising profiles.

At the time this policy was published, Fotolic did not require an account and did not use image-content advertising or a remote image-processing service.

04

Permissions

iOS requests permission before Fotolic accesses protected features. You can change these permissions in iOS Settings.

PhotosSelect photos to edit or save finished images.
CameraTake a photo when you choose the camera workflow.
FilesImport or export files through the system file picker.
NotificationsOptional local reminders, including a trial-end reminder if enabled.
05

Purchases and subscriptions

Fotolic Pro purchases are completed through Apple’s App Store and StoreKit. Apple processes payment information and provides the transaction status needed to manage access. Fotolic does not receive your payment card number.

Apple displays the price, billing period, introductory offer, trial eligibility, renewal terms, and cancellation instructions before purchase. Manage or cancel a subscription in your Apple Account settings. Apple’s handling of App Store information is described in Apple’s Privacy Policy.

06

Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or disclose your image content for advertising. When you use Share, Save to Photos, Save to Files, or another export action, iOS transfers the selected output to the destination you choose.

We may disclose support information if required by law, to protect the rights and safety of users, or to address fraud or security issues.

07

Retention and deletion

Your photos, files, exports, and local preferences remain on your device until you delete them, clear the app’s data, or uninstall Fotolic. Support correspondence is retained only as long as reasonably necessary to respond and maintain a support record.

08

Your choices

You can refuse or revoke permissions, delete local files, change settings, and manage subscriptions through iOS. Depending on where you live, you may also have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or information about processing. Contact us to make a request.

09

Children’s privacy

Fotolic is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information through support, contact us.

10

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when Fotolic’s features or legal requirements change. We will post the revised policy on this page and update the date above. Material changes may also be identified in the app or release notes.
