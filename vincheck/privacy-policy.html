VIN Check: Car Loan Deduction

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 26, 2026

Privacy PolicyTerms of Use

In short: no account, no ads, no analytics, and we run no servers that receive your data. Your loan and payment details stay on your device. Looking up a car sends its VIN to a U.S. government database, and finding car photos sends the year, make and model to a third-party image website.

1. Who we are

VIN Check: Car Loan Deduction ("VIN Check", the "app") is made by Sajid Khokhar, an independent developer ("we"). This policy explains what happens to information when you use the app.

2. Information stored on your device

The following is saved only on your device. We cannot see it.

  • VINs you check, their decoded vehicle details and results
  • Cars you save, and loan details such as lender name, amount, rate, term and start date
  • Payments you log or read from a statement
  • Filing status, income range and tax bracket you choose for estimates
  • App settings, reminder time, purchase status and saved links to car photos

If you back up your device with iCloud or a computer, this data may be part of that backup, under Apple's terms.

3. Information sent from your device

  • VIN lookup: the VIN, and sometimes the model year, is sent to the U.S. National Highway Traffic Safety Administration (NHTSA) vehicle database at vpic.nhtsa.dot.gov.
  • Car photos: the app searches a third-party image website using the car's model year, make and model, then loads the images. Your VIN, loan and payment details are not sent.
  • Purchases: Apple processes payments. We never receive your card details.
  • Links: pages you open from the app, such as IRS guidance, are loaded from their own websites.

As with any internet connection, these services can receive your IP address and basic technical information, and handle it under their own policies: U.S. Department of Transportation (NHTSA), the image website (under its own privacy policy) and Apple. VIN Check is not affiliated with or endorsed by any of them.

4. Apple and the App Store

The app is downloaded from Apple's App Store, and purchases use Apple's in-app purchase system. Apple handles your Apple Account, payment details, downloads and any diagnostics you choose to share with developers under the Apple Privacy Policy:
https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

The app only learns from Apple whether a Pro purchase is active.

5. Camera, photos and notifications

  • Camera: used to scan a VIN barcode and to read a loan statement. This processing happens on your device, and the app does not upload the images.
  • Photos: if you choose a statement image, the app reads only the one you pick, on your device.
  • Notifications: payment reminders are optional and scheduled on your device.

6. PDF summaries

The year-end PDF is created on your device. It leaves your device only if you share or save it somewhere yourself.

7. What we do not do

We do not collect your name, contacts or location, we do not use advertising or analytics tools, we do not track you across apps, and we do not sell or rent personal information.

8. Why information is used

Information is used only to provide the features you ask for: decoding a VIN, showing car photos, estimating and tracking your loan, sending your reminders, unlocking purchases and answering your emails. Where the GDPR applies, we rely on performing our agreement with you, your consent (for camera and notifications, which you can withdraw in iOS Settings) and our legitimate interest in running and supporting the app.

9. Security and international transfers

Lookups use encrypted (HTTPS) connections, and data on your device is protected by your device's own security, so keep a passcode on it. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. NHTSA, the image website and Apple may process requests in the United States or other countries.

10. Keeping and deleting data

Data stays on your device until you remove it. Settings → Delete all data removes saved cars, loans, payments, your tax profile and stored VIN results, and turns off reminders. Some small items, such as app preferences, the record of your free checks and saved photo links, remain until you delete the app. Deleting the app removes its data from your device; backups follow your backup settings.

11. If you email us

We receive your email address and whatever you write, and use it only to answer you. You can ask us to delete the conversation.

12. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information. Your app data is in your hands on your device. For anything you have emailed us, contact us. We do not sell or share personal information as defined by the CCPA, and we will not treat you differently for using your rights. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

13. Children

VIN Check is rated 4+ on the App Store and is suitable for all ages. It requires no account or sign-up, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

14. Changes

We may update this policy. The new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
