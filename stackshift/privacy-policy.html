STACK SHIFT

Privacy Policy

Stack Shift is a puzzle game. Here is what it uses, why it uses it, and how you stay in control, in plain language.

Effective date November 4, 2026Last updated November 4, 2026

At a glance

  • No sign-up. You never give us your name, email, or phone number to play.
  • Your game stays on your iPhone. Levels, stars, coins, gems, and settings are saved on your device.
  • Ads keep the game free. Google AdMob shows them, and you decide whether to allow tracking.
  • Firebase keeps the game healthy. It tells us about crashes and how the game is played, using anonymous identifiers.
  • Notifications are optional. Say yes or no, and change your mind any time.
  • Apple handles payments and Game Center. We never see your card details.
  • No access to your personal content. Stack Shift does not use your camera, microphone, photos, contacts, or precise location.
01

Who we are

This policy applies to the Stack Shift iOS app and its legal pages. “Stack Shift,” “we,” “us,” and “our” refer to Sajid A Khokhar, the developer of Stack Shift, who is responsible for the information handled as described here.

02

Information we use

Apart from support messages, which you send yourself, this information is collected automatically as you play, through the app and the services named in this policy.

Game dataLevels, stars, scores, coins, gems, boosters, themes, block skins, quests, your chosen avatar, and settings. Saved in the app’s private storage on your iPhone. We do not receive a copy.
Game CenterIf you are signed in, the app uses your Game Center name, photo, and player ID, plus your scores (total stars, highest level, and Rush scores). If you allow it, the app also shows your Game Center friends.
Advertising dataYour IP address, approximate location, device and app details, ad interactions, and your advertising identifier if you allow tracking. Collected by Google. See Ads and tracking.
Usage dataGame events, such as levels started and finished, screens viewed, features and boosters used, ads watched, and purchases made, with device model, iOS version, app version, language, and approximate region. See Analytics and crashes.
Crash dataTechnical details about the app and device at the moment of a crash.
Anonymous identifiersRandom IDs for your installation, created by Firebase. They are not linked to your name, email, or Apple Account.
Notification tokenA device token that lets us deliver notifications to this app, if you allow them.
Purchase detailsApple tells the app what you bought and when, so it can deliver your items. Records of purchases are kept with your game data.
Support messagesIf you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in the message.

04

Permissions

Every permission is optional, and declining one never stops you from playing. No feature, reward, or purchase depends on saying yes. You can change your choice later in iOS Settings > Apps > Stack Shift.

NotificationsUsed for things like Daily Challenge reminders, rewards ready to claim, events, and game news, which may include offers. They are sent through Apple Push Notification service and Firebase Cloud Messaging. If you decline, you simply will not get them.
Tracking (App Tracking Transparency)Asked after you finish the first tutorial. If you allow it, your advertising identifier (IDFA) can be used to show more relevant ads and to measure them. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, it is not accessed, and you still see ads, just less personalized ones.
Game Center friendsAsked when the app needs your friends list, so you can compare scores. If you decline, leaderboards still work.

Stack Shift also connects to Game Center when it opens if you are signed in on your iPhone. You can sign out in iOS Settings > Game Center. The Invite button only opens the iOS share sheet, and you choose where that message goes.

05

Ads and tracking

The free game shows ads from Google AdMob: optional rewarded videos, such as extra moves, and occasional full-screen ads between levels. Rewarded videos stay optional, even if you remove the other ads with a purchase.

To serve, limit, and measure ads, Google may collect the advertising data listed above. Where the law requires it, including in the EEA, the UK, and some US states, you see a consent message first. When that applies, you can change your answer at any time in Settings > Privacy Choices inside the game.

We ask Google to show only ads suitable for general audiences, and Stack Shift holds no health or other sensitive data to share with advertisers. No filter is perfect, so if you see an ad that seems inappropriate, use the options on the ad, or report it through Settings > Contact Support.

Learn more in how Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google’s Privacy Policy.

06

Analytics and crashes

We use these Google Firebase services:

Google Analytics for FirebaseRecords the usage data listed above, so we can see how the game is played and where it needs work. The advertising identifier is included only if you allow tracking.
Firebase CrashlyticsSends a crash report if the app stops unexpectedly, so we can fix the problem.
Firebase AuthenticationSigns in anonymously and automatically to give your installation a random ID. There is no username or password, and nothing for you to do.
Firebase Cloud MessagingDelivers notifications if you allow them.

Google processes this information for us as a service provider. You can turn analytics off at any time in Settings > Privacy Choices in the game, and the game works the same. Learn more in Firebase’s privacy information.

07

Purchases

All in-app purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your payment card details. Apple only confirms what you bought, so the game can deliver it. Apple’s handling of this information is described in Apple’s Privacy Policy.

08

Sharing

We only share information with the companies that make the game work:

AppleGame Center, App Store purchases, and push notification delivery.
GoogleAdMob advertising and Firebase services.

Apple and Google provide the same or equal protection of your data as described in this policy and as required by Apple’s App Store rules, under their contracts and privacy terms with us. We do not share your personal data with third-party AI services.

Other Game Center players can see your Game Center name, photo, and scores on leaderboards. We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect users, the game, or our rights.

We do not sell your personal information for money. Some US state laws treat personalized advertising as a “sale” or “sharing” of information. You can opt out by declining tracking or using Privacy Choices.

Apple and Google may process information in the United States and other countries, under safeguards for international transfers, such as standard contractual clauses.

09

Keeping and deleting

Game dataStays on your iPhone until you delete the app, which deletes it permanently.
Analytics and crash dataKept only as long as needed, according to our Firebase retention settings, then deleted automatically.
Game Center dataManaged by Apple through your Apple Account.
Support messagesKept only as long as reasonably needed to help you.

Deleting the app does not delete data already held by Google. To ask us to delete it, email us, and we will respond within 30 days. Because these identifiers are anonymous, we may need a detail from the app to find your data, and we will tell you what to send.

10

Your choices and rights

  • Tracking: iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Ad consent and analytics: Settings > Privacy Choices in the game.
  • Notifications: iOS Settings > Notifications > Stack Shift.
  • Game Center: iOS Settings > Game Center.
  • Everything on your device: delete the app.
  • Data held by Google: email us to request deletion.

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or move your information, to object to some uses, and to withdraw consent. You may also complain to your local data protection authority. Email us to make a request, and we will never treat you differently for doing so. Some US states also let you appeal our decision by replying to it. We do not use your data for automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects.

11

Children’s privacy

Stack Shift is rated 4+ on the App Store, which means its content is suitable for all ages. It is a general-audience game and is not specifically directed to children. Stack Shift does not ask for your age, has no chat, and has no way to post photos or messages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, or under the minimum age set by local law.

A parent or guardian can turn off tracking in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking, and can block in-app purchases with Screen Time. If you believe a child has given us personal information, for example by emailing support, contact us and we will delete it.
