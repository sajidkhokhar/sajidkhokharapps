About this policy

PerClick is a camera and photoshoot app developed by Sajid Khokhar. This policy explains how its current features handle photos, camera access, inspiration searches, and other information. No account is required.

02 /

Photos and information on your device

Captured photos, original and processed images, photo booth prints, session information, saved inspiration favorites, and capture preferences are stored locally on your device. Camera capture, photo looks, and booth rendering are processed on your device. PerClick does not upload your captured photos to developer-operated servers.

Reference-image screening uses Apple Vision on your device to detect people and image layouts. PerClick does not use this processing to identify people or create identity profiles.

The current app has no developer-operated accounts, advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or third-party crash-reporting SDKs. We do not sell your personal information or use your captured photos for advertising or AI model training.

03 /

Permissions

Camera: used to show the preview and capture photos when you use the camera.

Add to Photos: used to save photos and booth prints to your photo library when you request it or enable automatic saving. PerClick does not request access to read your full photo library.

The current app does not request microphone, contacts, or location permissions. You can change camera and Photos permissions in iOS Settings. Denying a permission limits the related feature.

04 /

Online inspiration and third-party services

Inspo loads search results from Pinterest and downloads reference images from its image hosts, including pinimg.com. Category searches and any search text you enter are sent to Pinterest. These services receive your IP address, request timing, browser or device information included in network requests, and the requested search or image URL. Avoid entering private or sensitive information in searches.

Search pages run in a non-persistent web session. Cookies and website storage may be used during that session; PerClick does not persist that web session’s cookies across sessions. Downloaded reference images and search results are cached on your device. Third-party pages may load additional resources under their providers’ policies.

Pinterest handles information according to its Privacy Policy and Cookie Policy. PerClick is not affiliated with or endorsed by Pinterest. Their retention and processing practices are outside our control.

When you save to Photos or use the system share sheet, Apple or your chosen destination handles the exported copy under its own settings and policies. Apple device backups or iCloud Photos may include data depending on your settings; PerClick does not operate a cloud-sync service.

These legal pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. Visiting them sends ordinary web request information to GitHub; see GitHub’s Privacy Statement.

05 /

Support messages

If you email support.sajidapps@gmail.com, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to respond, troubleshoot, and manage the request. Do not send photos or sensitive information unless needed. Email is handled by our email provider under its policies. We retain support correspondence only as needed for the request and legitimate recordkeeping or legal obligations.

06 /

Retention and deletion

Local sessions remain until you delete them or remove the app. You can delete individual photos or sessions, delete all sessions in Settings, remove saved inspiration favorites, and clear the Inspo cache. Search-result caches are refreshed after about 12 hours; downloaded images may remain until the cache is cleared or the system removes them.

Deleting app data does not delete copies saved to Photos, shared elsewhere, or stored in device backups. Manage those copies in their respective apps and backup settings. Removing the app removes its local container; restored backups may restore data. There is no PerClick server account to delete.

07 /

Security and your choices

PerClick uses the iOS app sandbox and HTTPS for its configured online services. No device or network transmission is completely secure. Keep your device protected and back up photos you want to keep.

Where applicable law grants access, correction, deletion, objection, or other privacy rights, contact us to exercise them for information we hold, such as support correspondence. We cannot access your locally stored photos or control third-party records. Requests concerning Pinterest, Apple, or GitHub should also be directed to those providers. Applicable rights and complaint options remain unaffected.

08 /

Children

PerClick is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect children’s personal information through support. If you believe a child has provided information to us, contact us so we can investigate and delete it where appropriate. Online inspiration is third-party content and may not be suitable for every age.

09 /

Changes and contact

We will update this page and its date when our practices change. Material changes will be communicated through appropriate app notices or release information.
