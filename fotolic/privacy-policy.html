Privacy by default

At the time of publication, Fotolic does not require an account and does not upload the images you choose. Image operations are designed to run locally on your Apple device.

01

Data we process

Fotolic processes information only to provide the feature you use.

▧

Photos and files

Images you choose from Photos, Files, or the camera are used for the requested edit. They are not sent to Fotolic servers.

⌁

App preferences

Settings such as output format, quality, appearance, haptics, and onboarding completion are stored locally on your device.

✓

Support messages

If you email support, we receive the address and information you include so we can respond to you.

02

On-device processing

Resize, compress, convert, crop, adjustments, filters, watermarking, batch jobs, PDF creation, and export are designed to run on your device. Temporary working data stays in the app sandbox and is removed by the operating system or the app’s cleanup routines when no longer needed.

Originals are preserved.Fotolic works on a working copy and exports only when you choose.
No photo training.We do not use your images to train models or build advertising profiles.
No account required.You can use the core workflows without creating a Fotolic account.
03

Permissions you control

iOS asks before Fotolic accesses protected features. You can grant or revoke access in Settings at any time.

PhotosChoose photos to edit or save finished images.
CameraTake a photo when you choose the camera workflow.
FilesImport or export images and documents using the system file picker.
NotificationsOptional local reminders, such as a trial-end reminder, if you enable them.
04

Subscriptions and purchases

Fotolic Pro purchases are completed through Apple’s App Store and StoreKit. Apple processes payment information and provides the transaction status needed to unlock your purchase. Fotolic does not receive your payment card details.

Eligibility, price, free-trial length, renewal, and cancellation terms are shown by Apple before purchase. You can manage or cancel a subscription in your Apple Account settings. See Apple’s Privacy Policy for Apple’s handling of App Store data.

05

Sharing and retention

We do not sell, rent, or share your photo content for advertising. We do not operate a remote image-processing service. When you use Share, Save to Photos, Save to Files, or another export action, iOS sends the selected output to the destination you choose.

Local files and preferences remain on your device until you delete them, clear the app’s data, or uninstall Fotolic. Support correspondence is retained only as long as reasonably needed to answer your request and maintain a support record.

06

Your choices and rights

You can access, edit, export, or delete your images and local preferences through iOS and Fotolic. You can also refuse or later revoke permissions. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or an explanation of processing. Email us and we will review the request.

07

Children’s privacy

Fotolic is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us through support, contact us so we can address it.

08

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when Fotolic’s features or legal requirements change. The “Last updated” date above will change with each revision. If a change is material, we may also highlight it in the app or in release notes.
