Introduction
Axio Money does not collect, transmit, or store your personal financial data on any server. There is no account to create and no cloud sync: everything you enter, scan or say stays on your device. This policy explains the limited technical data we do collect, the permissions the app asks for, and why.
1. Information We Collect
Usage analytics. We collect anonymized data about how the app is used — such as app opens, screens and features used, device type, OS version, and locale — via Firebase Analytics. Event details never include your transactions, amounts, titles, notes, or scanned or spoken content. Firebase assigns an app-instance identifier; the advertising identifier is only used if you allow tracking when asked.
Crash reports. If the app crashes, an anonymized report is sent via Firebase Crashlytics to help us diagnose and fix issues. Crash reports do not contain your financial data.
Push notification token. If you allow notifications, Apple and Firebase Cloud Messaging provide a device token so notifications can be delivered. It is not linked to your financial data.
Anonymous authentication & remote configuration. The app signs in to Firebase anonymously (no name, email, or password) and reads app settings, such as the free usage limit and update notices, from Firebase. This sends none of your financial data.
Subscription status. Purchase receipts are validated with Apple via StoreKit to manage your premium access. We do not store payment information.
Support email. If you contact us for support, we receive your email address solely to respond to your inquiry.
We do not collect names, financial data, location, contacts, photos, audio, or any personally identifiable information during normal app use.
2. Data Stored on Your Device Only
The following data is entered by you and stored locally on your device only. We have no access to it.
Expenses, income, categories, and notes.
Accounts and wallets, budgets, tags, financial goals and savings objectives.
Results of Scan and Voice entries. Photos you scan and audio you record are processed on the spot and are not saved or uploaded by the app.
AI chat history and generated insights.
Profile preferences including currency, theme, avatar, and notification settings.
None of this data leaves your device unless you explicitly export it yourself using the in-app export feature.
3. AI, Scan & Voice
On-device only. All AI features — including Axie AI, spending insights, savings tips, and understanding Scan and Voice entries — use Apple Foundation Models and run entirely on your device. Receipt and screenshot text is read on your device with Apple's Vision framework. Your financial data is never sent to external AI servers or third parties.
Device requirement. AI features require a compatible Apple device that supports Apple Foundation Models. On unsupported devices, these features are simply unavailable and no data is collected.
Voice. Speech recognition runs on your device where your language supports it. Otherwise iOS may use Apple's speech service to process the audio, under Apple's privacy policy. Axio Money does not keep the audio.
Chat history. AI conversation history is stored locally on your device and is permanently deleted when you use Delete Account in Settings.
4. Permissions We Request
Axio Money asks for a permission only when you use the feature that needs it. Every permission is optional and can be changed in iOS Settings → Axio Money. Declining affects only the related feature.
Notifications. Budget alerts when you approach or exceed a budget, and an optional daily reminder to log your expenses. These are scheduled on your device. The app also registers for Apple Push Notification service (delivered through Firebase Cloud Messaging) so we can send occasional announcements. Optional: change anytime in iOS Settings → Notifications.
App Tracking Transparency. Asks whether Axio Money may use your device's advertising identifier for app-performance measurement through Firebase Analytics. Optional: if you decline, every feature works exactly the same. Axio Money shows no ads and does not sell or share your data for advertising.
Camera. Used by Scan to read receipts, bills, salary slips and barcodes so the app can fill in an expense or income for you. Text is recognized on your device using Apple's Vision framework. Photos are not uploaded and are not saved by the app; only the transaction you keep is stored.
Photos (system photo picker). Lets you pick an existing receipt or bank/UPI screenshot for Scan. The app can only read the specific images you choose; it never receives access to your photo library.
Microphone. Used by Voice to listen while you say an expense or income, such as “spent 450 on groceries.” Audio is processed only while recording and is not stored by Axio Money.
Speech Recognition. Converts what you say into text for Voice. Where your language and device support it, recognition runs entirely on your device; otherwise iOS may process the audio using Apple's speech service, which is governed by Apple's privacy policy.
Face ID. Used to unlock accounts you have marked as Locked, keeping their transactions private. Biometric data stays inside Apple's Secure Enclave and is never available to Axio Money.
Siri, Shortcuts & Back Tap. App Intents let you add an expense or income from Shortcuts, Siri, the Action Button or Back Tap without opening the app. They run on your device and use no extra permission prompt.
Widgets. Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets read a summary of your data from storage shared with the app on your device. No permission prompt and nothing is sent anywhere.
Apple Intelligence (optional). Axie AI, spending insights and Scan/Voice understanding use Apple Foundation Models, an optional iOS feature on supported devices. It is not a permission; it runs on your device.
Axio Money does not request access to your location, contacts, calendars, health data, Bluetooth, or local network.
5. Third-Party Services
Firebase — Google LLC Privacy Policy
Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, Authentication (anonymous), Firestore (read-only remote configuration), and Cloud Messaging are used for anonymized analytics, crash reporting, anonymous sign-in, app settings, and notifications.
Apple — StoreKit Privacy Policy
Subscriptions are processed by Apple via StoreKit. Axio Money does not use iCloud or any other cloud sync for your data.
Axio Money is ad-free. We do not sell, share, or rent your personal information to advertisers or any third party. If you decline the tracking prompt, nothing about the app changes.
6. Your Controls
Access & Edit Available in app
Export Data CSV, JSON, PDF, Text, or Markdown
Delete Account Settings → Delete Account
Push Notifications iOS Settings → Notifications
Camera, Microphone, Speech, Face ID iOS Settings → Axio Money
Analytics Opt-Out iOS Settings → Privacy → Tracking
Deleting your account permanently erases all locally stored data including AI chat history. This action cannot be undone.
7. Children's Privacy
Axio Money is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at support.sajidapps@gmail.com and we will promptly delete it.
8. Data Retention

Firebase Analytics Up to 2 months
Firebase Crashlytics Up to 90 days

11. GDPR & CCPA Compliance
Axio Money is designed to comply with global privacy standards, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) in Europe and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) in the United States. Since we do not collect, transmit, or sell your personal financial data to third parties, we natively respect your rights to data minimization, privacy, and data portability. You can access, edit, export, or delete your data directly inside the app settings at any time.
